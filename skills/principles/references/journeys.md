# Journeys

Sources: G. Lynn Shostack, "Designing Services That Deliver", *Harvard Business Review* (Jan–Feb
1984) (service blueprints: the line of interaction, every step and hand-off); Jeff Patton, *User
Story Mapping* (O'Reilly, 2014) (the backbone: stages a user moves through, before, during and
after); Wharton, Rieman, Lewis & Polson, the cognitive walkthrough (1994), applied to every
action of a whole journey; Kahneman, Fredrickson, Schreiber & Redelmeier, "When more pain is
preferred to less: adding a better end", *Psychological Science* (1993) (peak–end rule).
Lou Downe, *Good Services: How to Design Services That Work* (BIS Publishers, 2020), its fifteen
principles of a good service, among them "have no dead ends", "make it easy to get human
assistance" and "clearly explain why a decision has been made".

| ID | Principle | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| `JOURNEY-ENTRY` | Each journey starts where the situation starts, with a visible entry point | An urgent journey that begins three menus deep; no way to start from the home screen |
| `JOURNEY-HANDOFF` | At every hand-off between screens the next step is obvious and the context carries over | Arriving on a screen that doesn't say why you're there; re-entering what you just gave |
| `JOURNEY-DEAD-END` | No state leaves the user without a way forward or back (Downe 2020: "have no dead ends") | A confirmation with no next step; a sheet that can't be closed |
| `JOURNEY-WAY-BACK` | From every step the user can get back to the start or home | Screens with no back, close or tab bar; home unreachable after a flow |
| `JOURNEY-CLOSE` | Loops close: the end of one stage leads into the next (after an order arrives, rate or reorder) | An "after" screen that ends without pointing to what to do next |
| `JOURNEY-BROKEN` | The journey can be completed with the screens that exist | A step the prototype doesn't offer; a control that does nothing |
| `JOURNEY-HELP` | When the journey can fail, a person can be reached from where it fails (Downe 2020) | An error or rejection with no contact route; help only in a settings menu |
| `JOURNEY-CONTEST` | A decision the app makes about the user (approve, reject, limit) says why and how to contest it (Downe 2020) | "Your application was declined" with no reason or next step |
| `JOURNEY-PEAK-END` | The most intense moment and the end of a journey are designed with care | A stressful step with no reassurance; an ending that is an empty screen |
