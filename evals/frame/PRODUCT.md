# Product

## Name

Sprout

## Platform

ios

## Stack

undecided

## Users

Everyone who owns plants, and their friends. [assumption]

## Product Purpose

A social app where plant owners keep streaks, earn badges and compare themselves with friends. [assumption]

## Problem

Plant owners need a leaderboard with streaks and badges, because without one they lose motivation and
forget to care for their plants. [assumption]

## Key Tasks

1. **Share my streak with friends** (must not fail): When I have kept a streak going, I want to post
   it, so my friends see it.
2. **Climb the leaderboard**: When I open the app, I want to see my rank among my friends.
3. **Water a plant**: When a plant needs water, I want to be told.

## Success Criteria

- Effectiveness: a streak can be shared in two taps.
- Efficiency: the leaderboard is the first thing on the home screen.
- Satisfaction: users feel proud of their rank.

## Operating Context

At home, a few times a week, often with wet hands. [assumption]
